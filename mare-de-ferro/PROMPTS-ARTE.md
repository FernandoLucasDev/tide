Método: ferramenta integrada image_gen. Imagens geradas por IA como exploração de direção de arte. Não representam modelos 3D, sprites, telas implementadas ou assets finais de produção. A imagem do porto serve principalmente como referência de atmosfera e materiais; a simplificação visual para o jogo está descrita no brief.

Original visual identity; do not reproduce any existing game.

## 02 — King-Crab King e a frota

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original game narrative key art, landscape. MARÉ DE FERRO: a fishing fleet attempting to capture an ancient colossal king crab during a midnight ocean storm. Elevated oblique view with one foreground 18-meter industrial crab fishing vessel and three smaller distant vessels forming a coordinated arc; taut winch cables connect to the giant's shell. The crab must be unmistakably a crab, with broad spiny rust-red carapace, two immense claws and articulated legs, partly submerged, old anchors, severed cables and a fragment of a wreck embedded in shell, barnacles and kelp; no human face, no fantasy crown. Its visible shell is larger than all the ships, but most of its body remains hidden under dark water. Foreground crew tiny in mustard waterproofs. Painterly stylized 3D concept art with gouache surface texture and readable simplified silhouettes, dramatic but consistent with cozy maritime village art direction. Petroleum blue and midnight indigo, cold seafoam surf, restrained amber deck lights, rusty coral monster. Awe, danger, ethical tension, no gore. Strong negative space of black ocean, cinematic landscape. No text, logos or watermark. Original identity, not a copy of any game.

## 03 — Criaturas dos covos

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original game creature design sheet, landscape, warm ivory paper background, six separate beautifully spaced full-body creature illustrations in a coherent painterly stylized 3D / gouache art direction for maritime game MARÉ DE FERRO. Top row: small rusty red crab wearing a dented cooking pot as armor with eyes peeking out; clever small purple octopus holding a brass key and a stolen fishing float; round grumpy inflated pufferfish caught in a loose rope loop, unhurt. Bottom row: tiny hermit crab using an old blue floral teacup as shell; sea slug glowing gentle seafoam green with delicate translucent frills; almost transparent deep-sea crab with a single tiny amber light inside its shell, mysterious but anatomically readable. Each has a charming distinct silhouette, believable marine body plan, tactile simple materials and expressive posture; playful but not childish, no giant cartoon eyes, no clothes except salvaged shells. Soft tiny ground shadows, consistent scale for collectible small marine creatures, palette petroleum teal, coral rust, mustard, seafoam and ivory. No grid lines, no labels, no letters, no logo, no watermark. Clean professional character exploration sheet, not pixel art, not photorealistic.
